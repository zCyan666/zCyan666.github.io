1. Who handles your information

This website describes the services of an independent software developer based in Wuhan, China. For privacy requests and project communications, contact zhoulianhao123@gmail.com. 2. Information you choose to share

If you email me, I receive the information you include, such as your name, email address, requirements, attachments, project correspondence, and invoice references. Only share information needed for the enquiry or project. Do not send passwords, full card details, or unnecessary sensitive data.

The contact form builds an email draft in your browser. It does not submit your enquiry to a website database or save the draft in browser storage. Your email provider handles the message after you choose to send it.

3. Website data and cookies

The website code does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or marketing cookies, and does not use local storage for enquiries. A local browser preference remembers your day/night theme. It does not include enquiry or project information. A hosting or access platform may set its own operational cookies; its own privacy rules apply.

Information is used to answer enquiries, prepare quotes, perform agreed services, provide support, manage invoices and refund requests, keep necessary business records, and protect systems against misuse. Where a legal basis is required, processing depends on the circumstances, such as steps toward a contract, contractual performance, legal obligations, or legitimate business interests. Consent is requested where required.

Information may be handled by email, hosting, code-repository, file-delivery, or payment providers when needed for the agreed work. This site is intended for people seeking professional software services, not for children. Please do not submit children’s personal information. Policy changes will be posted here with an updated date. Material changes affecting an existing project will be communicated when appropriate.
