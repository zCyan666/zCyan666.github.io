1. What this policy covers

This policy covers project-based Python scripts, automation, Qt desktop development, API integration, and related services supplied by the independent developer described on this website, based in Wuhan, China. Services are delivered digitally. No physical return or shipping is required.

2. Cancel before work begins

You may request cancellation by email. If no work has started, payments for the unstarted work will be refunded in full. Any separately approved third-party purchase must be disclosed and specifically authorized before it is incurred; cancellation of that purchase is subject to its provider’s terms and applicable law.

3. Cancel after work has started

If work has begun, the refund is the amount paid minus the documented value of work completed within the approved scope and any expressly approved, non-recoverable third-party costs. The allocation is based on the milestone amounts or rates in the accepted quote, with an itemized explanation. Unused amounts for work not performed are refundable. A cancellation does not create an automatic forfeiture of the entire deposit.

4. Delivery issues or incorrect work

If the delivered work does not meet the agreed scope, email a description of the issue and relevant examples. I will review the issue against the written specification and discuss correction, a proportionate refund, or another appropriate resolution. If agreed work cannot be delivered, amounts paid for undelivered work will be refunded. Report issues promptly; statutory rights are not limited by any project review period.

5. How to request a refund

Email zhoulianhao123@gmail.com with your name, project or invoice reference, payment date, and reason for the request. Please do not send card numbers or passwords. I aim to acknowledge requests within two business days and provide an initial review within five business days. If more information is needed, I will explain the next steps.

6. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where supported. The payment provider or bank controls when funds appear in your account, so the posting date cannot be guaranteed. You will receive confirmation when the refund is initiated. Any payment-provider fee treatment follows the provider’s rules and applicable law; no undisclosed cancellation fee is imposed by this policy.

7. Your other rights

This policy does not exclude mandatory consumer rights, or any dispute, chargeback, or buyer-protection process available under applicable law or your payment provider’s terms. Any project-specific cancellation arrangement must be disclosed and accepted before payment and must respect those rights.
