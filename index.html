04 / GOOD TO KNOW

More questions? Get in touch ↗

How is the final price decided?

The ranges are indicative estimates in USD. After reviewing your requirements, I provide a written quote covering the scope, total price, currency, applicable taxes, milestones, and any approved external costs. You approve it before paid work begins.

How will I receive my software?

Delivery is digital, through an agreed repository, download link, or email. Your proposal specifies the deliverables, supported operating system, source-code arrangement, installation instructions, and acceptance criteria. There is no physical shipping.

Can I pay through PayPal?

Payment methods are confirmed in your written proposal. If PayPal invoicing is agreed and available, an invoice is issued separately. This website does not process payments, collect card details, or start subscriptions.

What if my requirements change?

We agree on any change to the scope, price, or schedule in writing before the additional work begins. You can review the impact before deciding.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Before work begins, payments for work that has not started are refundable. If work has begun, completed work and any pre-approved, non-recoverable third-party costs are accounted for, and the unused balance is refunded. See the refund and cancellation policy for details. Applicable consumer and payment-provider rights are unaffected.

Do you provide support after delivery?

The quote defines the review period and any included fixes for issues against the agreed scope. Ongoing maintenance, new features, or support beyond that scope are quoted separately. Please email the support address with your project reference.
