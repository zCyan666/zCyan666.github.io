Before you pay

Your quote lists the scope, total amount, billing currency, applicable taxes, deliverables, timeline, and payment stages. Any approved third-party costs are listed separately. A quote does not commit you to a purchase.

Payments & milestones

A deposit or milestone billing may be agreed for your project. Payment instructions are supplied only after scope approval. If PayPal invoicing is agreed and available, the invoice is sent separately; there is no live checkout on this website.

Digital handover

Software is delivered by email, repository, or a download link, as agreed. Supported operating systems, source-code rights, external licenses, review periods, and included support are specified in the proposal. No physical shipping is involved.

Changes & cancellations

Additional work requires an agreed change to the quote. Unused payments are handled under the refund policy. No recurring subscription is started by this website.
